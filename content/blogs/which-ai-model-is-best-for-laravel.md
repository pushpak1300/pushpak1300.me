This Laravel article covers Boost Benchmarks, an evaluation framework that runs AI coding agents against real Laravel tasks and verifies their output with Pest tests.

It benchmarks haiku 4.5, sonnet 4.6, opus 4.6, kimi k2.5, gpt-5.3 codex, and gpt-5.4 across 17 evaluations, with and without Laravel Boost, and breaks down which model wins on speed, cost, and correctness.
